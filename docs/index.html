Calculate Revenue and Filter by Coffee

sales["revenue"] = sales["units_sold"] * sales["unit_price"]

coffee = sales[sales["category"] == "Coffee"]

Plot

daily = coffee.groupby(
    ["date", "cafe"],
    as_index=False
)["revenue"].sum()

px.line(
    daily,
    x="date",
    y="revenue",
    color="cafe",
    markers=True,
    title="Daily Coffee Revenue by Cafe",
    labels={
        "date": "Date",
        "revenue": "Revenue ($)"
    }
)
NoteWhy I Filtered to Coffee

I filtered the data to coffee so I could compare revenue for the same product category across the three cafes without mixing in products with different prices and sales patterns.
